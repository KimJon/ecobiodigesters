About Eco Biodigesters

We help Kenyan families and landlords build modern, smell-free homes without the recurring expense of exhauster trucks.

Our Story & Promise

Practical, Clean Solutions That Actually Work

For years, Kenyan property owners had only one choice for handling toilet waste: digging a big, deep septic tank. But after a few years, the tank fills up, starts smelling terrible, and you are forced to pay KSh 10,000 to KSh 20,000 every few months for exhauster trucks.

At Eco Biodigesters, with our main physical office in Ruai Off Kangundo Road, we provide a modern alternative that saves your money and gives your family total peace of mind.

Our systems use friendly, natural bacteria in a strong 3-chamber tank. The bacteria digest and liquefy the waste so only clean, clear water flows out into an underground soak pit. No sludge buildup, no bad smell, no flies, and no exhauster bills.
