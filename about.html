Modern, eco-friendly waste management solutions for homes, apartments, and commercial properties across Kenya. Head office in Ruai Off Kangundo Road, Nairobi.

Who We Are

Reliable Waste Solutions That Never Fill Up

Eco Biodigesters was founded to provide property owners in Kenya with a permanent, modern alternative to outdated septic tanks. Traditional septic tanks fill up quickly, generate unpleasant odours, and require expensive exhauster trucks year after year.

Operating from our head office and demonstration facility in Ruai Off Kangundo Road, Nairobi, we have completed over 500 successful installations across Kenya. Our clients include private residential homeowners, apartment developers, commercial complexes, schools, and health facilities.

Our systems are constructed using reinforced polystyrene composite technology. By combining high-density polystyrene formwork with steel-reinforced plaster, we create strong, underground digestion tanks that resist ground movement, withstand heavy loads, and last for over 50 years without cracking or leaking.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions about our biodigester systems and installation process.

How does an Eco Biodigester work without filling up?

Waste from your toilets and drains enters the first underground chamber. Natural beneficial bacteria break down solid organic waste into clean water and harmless trace gas. The clear, treated water then flows through overflow pipes into a gravel-filled soakaway pit where it absorbs safely into the soil. Because solid waste is continuously digested into water, the tank never fills up.

How long does the installation take?

Standard residential installations are completed within 1 to 7 working days. Our full workflow includes site excavation, assembling the reinforced polystyrene structure, pipe connections, building the soakaway pit, testing, and clean compound backfilling.

Why is the system completely odour-free?

Our biodigesters are sealed completely airtight underground. Waste breaks down naturally without exposure to open air. Any trace gases vent safely through your existing building plumbing vent pipes above the roofline. With no open chambers, bad smells, flies, and mosquitoes are completely prevented.

Does the system work in black cotton soil and rocky areas?

Yes. In black cotton soil areas (such as Ruai, Joska, Kamulu, and Ruiru), we build high-capacity soakaway pits and French drains filled with clean drainage ballast and geotextile lining to ensure smooth water absorption. In rocky areas (like Kitengela), our team uses specialized rock breakers to achieve the required depth.

Can you convert my existing septic tank into a biodigester?

Yes. Converting an old septic tank is quick and cost-effective. We exhaust and clean the tank one final time, construct internal biological chambers, install overflow pipes, cast an airtight top slab, and introduce active bacterial enzymes. You will never need an exhauster truck again.

Where are your offices and which areas do you cover?

Our head office and demonstration facility are located in Ruai Off Kangundo Road, Nairobi. We deploy trained installation teams to projects across all 47 counties in Kenya, including Nairobi, Kiambu, Machakos, Kajiado, Nakuru, Eldoret, Kisumu, and Mombasa.
