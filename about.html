Corporate Profile

Pioneering Sustainable Sanitation Engineering

Eco Biodigesters was established to engineer viable, long-term alternatives to obsolete masonry septic tanks. Traditional septic systems frequently suffer from structural fracturing, sub-soil contamination, chronic fugitive odours, and crippling recurring expenditures on vacuum exhauster bowsers.

Operating from our primary operations and demonstration facility situated in Ruai Off Kangundo Road, Nairobi, Eco Biodigesters has successfully engineered and commissioned over 500 decentralized anaerobic treatment installations nationwide. Our client portfolio spans private residential developments, commercial multi-door rental complexes, educational institutions, healthcare centers, and industrial facilities.

Our core structural advantage lies in our reinforced expanded polystyrene (EPS) composite technology. By integrating high-density structural EPS panels with monolithic steel-reinforced mortar, we manufacture sub-surface bio-reactors that are impervious to ground movement, expansive clay heaving, and water ingress, delivering over 50 years of continuous structural integrity.

Frequently Asked Technical Questions

Detailed engineering explanations addressing the principles, execution, and reliability of our bio-digestion infrastructure.

What biological principles govern the operation of an Eco Biodigester?

An Eco Biodigester is a decentralized, sub-surface anaerobic bioreactor. Rather than serving as an open septic holding vault, it cultivates an active population of anaerobic microbial enzymes. These microorganisms metabolize complex organic matter into clarified liquid effluent and trace, inert biogas. The clarified effluent gravity-flows through multi-chamber inverted tees into an engineered ballast dispersal soakaway, recharging sub-surface soil moisture without environmental contamination.

What is the standard civil execution and commissioning timeframe?

Standard residential and light-commercial installations are completed within 1 to 7 working days. Our turnkey workflow encompasses mechanical or hand excavation, sub-grade foundation preparation, EPS composite formwork casting, hydraulic interconnection, pressure-testing, backfilling, and clean site restoration.

How does the installation guarantee 100% odour containment and vector control?

Odour in conventional septic tanks is produced by stagnant, uncontrolled aerobic putrefaction exposed to atmospheric oxygen. In our hermetically sealed anaerobic bioreactors, organic breakdown occurs in an airtight environment. Residual trace gases are vented safely above building roofline via standardized building plumbing vents. With zero open chambers, flies, mosquitoes, and vermin are completely excluded.

How are soakaway dispersal arrays engineered for low-percolation soils (e.g. Black Cotton)?

In regions characterized by dense, expansive montmorillonite clay (black cotton soil) such as Ruai, Joska, Kamulu, and Ruiru, soil percolation rates are low. Our engineering protocol involves excavating high-volume dispersal arrays backfilled with washed volcanic rock ballast and lined with non-woven geotextile fabric. In extensive commercial sites, we integrate sub-surface perforated French drain trenches that disperse treated effluent over a broad horizontal absorption area.

What is the technical feasibility of retrofitting an existing traditional septic tank?

Retrofitting is completely feasible and highly cost-effective. Following a final vacuum evacuation and decontamination wash, our civil team constructs interior partition walls to establish anaerobic digestion chambers, installs inverted hydraulic overflow manifolds, casts a reinforced hermetic slab, and seeds the chambers with concentrated bacterial cultures. This permanently eliminates the need for future vacuum tanker visits.

What is your geographical operational scope and corporate location?

Our central operations facility and corporate demonstration center are situated in Ruai Off Kangundo Road, Nairobi. We maintain dedicated mobile civil installation crews capable of deploying to project sites across all 47 counties in Kenya, including Kiambu, Machakos, Kajiado, Nakuru, Eldoret, Kisumu, Kilifi, and Mombasa.
