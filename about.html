Who We Are

Building Kenya's Clean Sanitation Future

Eco Biodigesters was founded to solve a very common and frustrating problem facing Kenyan property owners: smelly, overflowing traditional septic tanks that require endless, expensive trips by exhauster bowser trucks.

Operating from our physical office located in Ruai Off Kangundo Road, Nairobi, we specialize in constructing modern biological digesters using proven reinforced polystyrene technology. Our systems decompose toilet waste naturally through anaerobic bacteria, turning waste into clean soakage water that safely returns to the earth with zero bad smell.

With over 500 successful installations across black cotton soil in Ruai, Joska, Kamulu and Ruiru, rocky ground in Kitengela and Athi River, and red soil in Kiambu and Nakuru, we deliver permanent sanitation peace of mind in just 1 to 7 working days.

Frequently Asked Questions

Get clear, truthful answers to common questions about biodigester installation in Kenya.

What is an Eco Biodigester and how does it work?

An Eco Biodigester is an on-site, underground biological wastewater treatment tank. Instead of merely storing raw sewage like an old septic tank, it uses live anaerobic bacteria to break down solid toilet waste into treated water and harmless gases. The clear water safely exits into an engineered ballast soak pit and absorbs naturally into the earth.

How long does the installation take?

A standard residential biodigester is completed in just 1 to 7 working days, depending on ground conditions (rocky or soft soil) and the size of your compound. Our fundis work quickly and leave your compound backfilled and neat.

Will the system produce bad smells or attract flies?

No, absolutely not. The digestion process takes place in an airtight, underground chamber. Because the waste is decomposed biologically rather than left to rot stagnantly, there is zero foul smell, zero flies, and zero mosquitoes.

Can a biodigester work in black cotton soil or rocky terrain?

Yes! We have extensive experience in black cotton soil (such as Ruai, Joska, Kamulu, and Ruiru) and hard rock ground (such as Kitengela and Athi River). In black cotton, we construct wider French drain soakaways with coarse ballast and geotextile lining so water drains continuously even during heavy rains.

Can you convert our old traditional septic tank into a biodigester?

Yes! If you already have a traditional septic tank that is filling up constantly, we can empty it one last time, construct internal baffle chambers, seal it airtight, and introduce live bacterial enzymes. You don't have to demolish your compound to get the benefits of a modern biodigester.

Where is your physical office and do you serve outside Nairobi?

Our physical office is located in Ruai Off Kangundo Road, Nairobi. We provide countrywide installation across all 47 counties in Kenya including Kiambu, Machakos, Kajiado, Nakuru, Eldoret, Kisumu, Mombasa, and beyond.
