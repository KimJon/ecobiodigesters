How Our 3-Chamber Biodigester Works

A completely natural, gravity-driven biological process that requires zero electricity, no noisy motors, and minimal upkeep.

1

Wastewater Inflow

Raw blackwater (toilets) and greywater (showers/sinks) enter the primary chamber via gravity. Solid waste settles at the bottom while grease and light particles float, allowing uniform separation.

Heavy solids naturally trapped
2

Anaerobic Digestion

Colonies of specialized anaerobic bacteria feed on the organic waste. They liquefy the solid faecal matter into clear bio-effluent and trace harmless gases, reducing waste volume by over 98%.

98%+ Solid organic reduction
3

Safe Soakaway Drainage

The fully digested, pathogen-free water flows through the final filtration chamber into an engineered gravel/ballast soakaway pit or French drain, soaking harmlessly back into the earth.

Clear, odourless earth soak

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about biodigester installation, maintenance, and operation in Kenya.

A traditional septic tank is merely a storage tank that collects raw sludge. Over time it fills up, requires frequent costly exhauster pumping, and generates foul sewage odours. An Eco Biodigester uses a 3-chamber biological process where active anaerobic bacteria continuously digest solid human waste into liquid effluent and water, reducing solid volume by 98% and soaking away safely without odour.
Under normal domestic usage, an Eco Biodigester practically never needs exhausting. Because bacteria continuously break down organic solids into liquid, only a negligible trace of inert mineral residue remains at the base after many years.
Yes! Standard biodegradable toilet paper breaks down easily inside the biodigester. Normal household toilet detergents (like regular soaps and Harpic in moderate amounts) are fine. However, you should avoid pouring excessive pure undiluted harsh chemicals, caustic acid, motor oil, or petroleum into your drains, as these could suppress the natural bacteria.
For residential homes (1 to 4 bedrooms), complete installation typically takes only 1 to 2 working days. Commercial or institutional installations for apartments and schools generally take 3 to 5 days depending on the capacity and soil conditions.
We specialize in difficult Kenyan terrains! For black cotton soil (which absorbs water slowly), we design an extended french drain or deep ballast soakaway that reaches porous permeable sub-layers. In rocky soils (like Kitengela), we excavate to the required gradient and build a horizontal dispersion drain packed with graded ballast rocks.
Yes, we do septic tank conversions regularly! We exhaust the old tank one last time, clean it thoroughly, install internal baffle division walls and biological media, seal it airtight, and inoculate it with high-potency bio-enzymes.
