Why Choose Eco Biodigesters?

Here is what makes our reinforced polystyrene system superior to traditional septic tanks.

No Nasty Odour

Airtight sealed tanks and biological anaerobic breakdown prevent foul smells completely.

No Exhausting Ever

Faecal solids are broken down into clean water that naturally drains through the soak pit. Never gets full!

Very Economical

One-time installation that saves you from paying thousands of shillings every few months to exhauster trucks.

Space Saving

Occupies a compact area of your compound and can be safely concealed under lawns, driveways, or parking.

All Terrain Types

Engineered for black cotton soil (Ruai, Joska, Ruiru), hard rock (Kitengela, Athi River), red loam, and murram.

50+ Year Durability

Reinforced polystyrene does not rust, rot, or degrade underground, guaranteeing lifelong sanitation performance.
