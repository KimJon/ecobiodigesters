What Determines Your Exact Quotation?

Unlike sales companies who give you random numbers online, we calculate an honest price based on your plot's exact reality:

Soil & Ground Conditions

Red loamy soil drains water naturally. Black cotton soil (common in Ruai, Joska, and Kamakis) or hard bedrock (Kitengela, Athi River) requires specialized soakaway sizing or a French drain bed.

Occupancy & Water Volume

A 3-bedroom retirement home produces very different daily wastewater compared to a 16-door bedsitter apartment block. We size the chamber volume to guarantee it never overflows.

Site Location Across Kenya
