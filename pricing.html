Civil Parameters Influencing Project Proposals

Because geological and hydraulic conditions vary across Kenyan terrains, our proposals are calculated transparently against verified site variables.

Geotechnical Soil Profile

Expansive black cotton clay (Ruai, Joska) requires extended ballast soakaways; dense volcanic rock (Kitengela) requires pneumatic breaker tooling.

Hydraulic Load Rating

Calculated against continuous occupant volume, peak plumbing discharge rates, and graywater versus blackwater separation requirements.

Hydraulic Fall & Conduit Distance

Distance from vertical building plumbing stacks to the bio-reactor, and from the bio-reactor to the final sub-surface soakaway array.

Logistical Deployment Scope

Operational mobilization across all 47 counties in Kenya. We deploy certified civil engineering supervisors and equipment directly to site.

Turnkey Lump-Sum Integrity

All-inclusive engineering proposals: mechanical excavation, EPS composite formwork, Class 41 PVC lines, rock ballast, and bacterial inoculation.

Substantial Lifecycle ROI

A single capital infrastructure investment that permanently terminates recurrent operational vacuum bowser evacuation overheads.
