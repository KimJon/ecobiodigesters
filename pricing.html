What Determines Your Site Quotation?

Because every plot in Kenya has different soil and layout, we tailor each quote fairly based on real site variables.

Soil & Terrain

Black cotton soil (Ruai, Joska) requires larger ballast soak pits; rocky ground (Kitengela) requires compressor breaker tools.

Occupancy & Load

A single family home needs a compact tank, while multi-door rental units or schools require dual or triple cell capacity.

Plumbing Distance & Fall

Distance from your toilet downpipes to the biodigester, and from the biodigester to the soakaway pit.

Location Across Kenya

We serve all 47 counties. We bring our experienced fundis and materials directly to your site, near or far.

Zero Hidden Charges

Our quotations are all-inclusive: materials, excavation, reinforced polystyrene, piping, ballast, and enzymes.

Lifetime Value

A permanent one-time investment that completely frees you from recurring exhauster bills for the next 50+ years.
