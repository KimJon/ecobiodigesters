What Determines Your Custom Quote?

Because soil conditions and property layouts vary, every quote is calculated transparently based on your site needs.

Soil Type

Black cotton soil (Ruai, Joska) requires larger ballast soak pits; rocky ground (Kitengela) requires rock breaker tools.

Number of Users

The tank size is calculated based on the number of bedrooms, permanent residents, or commercial daily visitors.

Pipe Distance & Gradient

The length of PVC pipe needed from your building toilets and drains to the biodigester and soakaway pit.

Project Location

We operate across all 47 counties in Kenya. Mobilization costs are calculated based on your county location.

All-Inclusive Package

Our quotes include excavation, materials, reinforced polystyrene tank, PVC piping, drainage stones, and bacterial enzymes.

One-Time Investment

A single capital investment that permanently eliminates recurrent exhauster truck expenses, saving you money for decades.
