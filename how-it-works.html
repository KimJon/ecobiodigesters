Principles of Anaerobic Bio-Digestion

Autonomous microbial decomposition. Hermetically sealed anaerobic digestion. Zero mechanical moving parts, zero electrical power requirements, and complete atmospheric containment.

The 3-Stage Biochemical Process

Unlike conventional masonry septic tanks that merely accumulate raw sludge in an open pit, an Eco Biodigester actively treats and liquefies organic effluent through continuous anaerobic biodegradation.

1

1. Primary Inflow & Gravimetric Stratification

Raw wastewater discharges from building plumbing into the primary anaerobic chamber through laser-aligned Class 41 PVC sewer conduits. Heavy organic solids settle to the anaerobic digestion zone while liquid effluent stratifies above.

2

2. Multi-Stage Anaerobic Microbial Digestion

Concentrated colonies of specialized anaerobic microorganisms systematically digest complex organic compounds in an oxygen-depleted environment. Over 95% of solid waste is decomposed into clarified liquid effluent and trace inert biogas.

3

3. Clarified Effluent Dispersal & Soil Recharge

Treated, pathogen-reduced liquid effluent gravity-overflows via directional inverted T-baffles into an engineered ballast soakaway array. Clarified water safely disperses into the sub-surface soil strata without environmental contamination.

Applied Engineering

The Science of 100% Atmospheric Odour Containment

Foul odours in conventional septic infrastructure stem from uncontrolled aerobic putrefaction exposed to atmospheric oxygen. This produces heavy concentrations of noxious hydrogen sulfide and ammonia gases.

In our reinforced expanded polystyrene (EPS) composite bio-reactors, the digestion process occurs in a hermetically sealed, sub-surface environment. Anaerobic metabolism produces minimal trace gases that vent harmlessly above building rooflines through standardized building plumbing stacks. With zero open access points, the facility remains entirely odourless and impervious to vermin or insect vectors.

  • Airtight reinforced composite containment eliminates fugitive atmospheric emissions
  • Zero mechanical pumps, electrical motors, or moving parts susceptible to mechanical fatigue
  • Self-sustaining biological microbial colony requiring minimal chemical intervention
  • Permanent sub-surface civil infrastructure engineered for 50+ years of operational durability

Brownfield Remediation

Modernizing Failing Traditional Septic Infrastructure

Demolition of existing compound infrastructure is costly, disruptive, and unnecessary. Our specialized civil engineering teams routinely retrofit obsolete, overflowing masonry septic tanks, converting them into high-performance multi-chamber bio-reactors.

  • Final mechanical vacuum evacuation and thorough high-pressure decontamination wash
  • Engineering of internal masonry baffle partitions to establish anaerobic cascade chambers
  • Installation of directional hydraulic tees to regulate horizontal effluent transfer
  • Structural casting of a reinforced hermetic top slab with flush-level inspection ports
  • Immediate biological activation using concentrated bacterial microbial enzyme cultures
  • Permanent elimination of recurrent vacuum tanker evacuation expenditures
