How an Eco Biodigester Works

Simple, natural science. No moving electrical parts, no noisy pumps, and no bad smell. Here is what happens underground.

Biological Treatment Process

How Toilet Waste Turns into Clean Water

Instead of just storing faecal sludge like an old septic tank, a biodigester treats the waste naturally.

1

Waste Enters Tank

When you flush the toilet or drain water, it flows through standard PVC pipes straight into the first chamber of the biodigester. Solid waste settles to the bottom while liquid stays on top.

Natural separation by gravity
2

Bacteria Digest the Waste

Inside the airtight chambers, colonies of friendly bacteria get to work. They feed on the human waste and break it down into clean water and trace harmless gases. Over 98% of solid matter disappears!

98% Solid waste eliminated
3

Clean Water Soaks Away

The treated, smell-free water flows through the final filter into an underground soak pit filled with clean volcanic ballast stones. The water harmlessly absorbs into the surrounding soil.

100% Odour-free drainage
