How It Works

A simple, natural 3-step biological process that breaks down waste underground. No odour. No exhauster trucks. Zero electricity.

The 3-Step Process

Unlike traditional septic tanks that simply store raw waste until they overflow, an Eco Biodigester actively breaks down waste into clean water.

1

1. Waste Enters the Tank

Wastewater from toilets, bathrooms, and sinks flows through heavy-duty PVC pipes directly into the first underground chamber. Solid waste settles at the bottom while water stays on top.

2

2. Natural Biological Breakdown

Beneficial anaerobic bacteria digest and liquefy the solid organic waste inside airtight underground chambers. Over 95% of solid waste is converted into clean water and harmless trace gas.

3

3. Clean Water Absorption

Treated, clean water flows through overflow pipes into a gravel-filled soakaway pit. The water absorbs naturally and safely into the ground without smell or environmental contamination.

Clean & Odour-Free

Why Our Biodigesters Never Smell

Unpleasant odours in traditional septic tanks come from waste rotting in open air. This creates bad smells, draws flies and mosquitoes, and poses health risks to your household.

In our reinforced polystyrene biodigesters, waste breaks down inside a completely sealed underground system. Any harmless vent gases escape safely through your existing building plumbing vent pipes above the roofline. With no open chambers, your compound remains 100% odour-free, clean, and safe for everyone.

  • Airtight underground design prevents bad odours, flies, and mosquitoes
  • Zero electric motors or pumps — works 100% by natural gravity and biology
  • Self-sustaining bacteria colony requiring no expensive ongoing chemicals
  • Permanent underground structure built for 50+ years of trouble-free use

Upgrades & Conversions

Upgrading Old Septic Tanks into Modern Biodigesters

If you have an existing septic tank that fills up often or produces bad smells, you do not have to demolish your compound. Our team can convert your existing tank into a modern, odour-free biodigester.

  • We exhaust and clean the old tank for the last time
  • We build internal dividing walls to create biological treatment chambers
  • We install overflow pipes for smooth water drainage
  • We cast an airtight reinforced concrete slab with neat inspection covers
  • We add active bacterial enzymes to immediately start natural digestion
  • Permanent solution — no more paying for exhauster trucks
