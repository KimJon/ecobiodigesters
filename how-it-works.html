How an Eco Biodigester Works

Natural biological breakdown. No mechanical pumps, no electrical power needed, and no bad smell. Here is what happens underground.

The 3-Step Biological Process

Unlike a traditional septic tank that merely stores rotting raw sewage, an Eco Biodigester actively treats and digests wastewater naturally.

1

1. Wastewater Enters Tank

Toilet flushes and bathroom water flow through standard 4-inch heavy-duty PVC pipes directly into the first chamber. Heavy solids settle naturally to the bottom while light greywater stays on top.

2

2. Biological Digestion

Millions of active anaerobic bacteria feed on the organic solids. In this sealed, oxygen-free chamber, over 95% of the faecal sludge is digested into clear water and harmless nitrogen gas.

3

3. Clean Water Soakaway

The treated, pathogen-free liquid effluent overflows via T-baffles into an engineered ballast soak pit. Clean water drains harmlessly into the earth. It never backs up or fills the tank!

Engineered Science

Why an Eco Biodigester Never Smells

Conventional septic tanks stink because raw sewage is exposed to trapped air and starts decomposing through uncontrolled aerobic putrefaction.

With our reinforced polystyrene technology, the tank is completely airtight. Anaerobic bacteria break down waste without pungent hydrogen sulfide gas. The tiny amount of odorless gas produced naturally vents above roof level through your plumbing stack pipe.

  • Airtight reinforced polystyrene chambers prevent smell escape
  • No mechanical pumps, no electricity bills, no moving parts to break
  • Naturally self-sustaining colony of active digestive enzymes
  • Permanent solution engineered to last 50+ years underground
