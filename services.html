Our Services

Reliable, permanent waste management and drainage solutions tailored for homes, apartments, and commercial properties across Kenya.

Our Core Service

Biodigester Installation

Permanent, underground waste systems that use natural biological digestion to treat waste into clean water. Built with reinforced polystyrene composite technology, our systems never fill up and produce zero odour.

  • Never fills up — zero exhauster truck visits needed
  • 100% odour-free with airtight sealed chambers
  • 50+ year durability with reinforced polystyrene technology
  • Engineered for all Kenyan soils (black cotton, rock, red soil)
  • Fast 1 to 7 working days installation timeline
  • Compact underground design safe under lawns, driveways, or parking

Kitchen Wastewater Solutions

Commercial Grease Traps

Cooking fats, oils, and food grease from kitchen sinks can solidify and block your sewer lines or choke your soak pit. Our grease interceptors separate fats from water before it enters your drainage system.

  • Prevents severe drainage blockages and pipe clogging
  • Removable lid for easy, routine cleaning
  • Essential for restaurants, hotels, schools, and busy homes
  • Custom-sized to handle your kitchen's daily volume
  • Protects your soakaway pit from fatty grease build-up

Upgrades & Repairs

Septic Tank Conversion

Do you have an existing septic tank that fills up frequently or smells bad? You do not have to demolish your compound. Our team converts existing tanks into modern, odourless biodigesters.

  • We exhaust and clean the old tank for the last time
  • We build internal biological division walls inside the tank
  • We install overflow pipes for smooth water drainage
  • We cast an airtight top slab with inspection lids
  • We add active bacterial enzymes to start natural digestion
  • Permanent fix — no more exhauster bills

Plumbing & Drainage Services

Proper plumbing is essential for any waste system. We handle complete trenching, pipe laying with correct slope angles, inspection manholes, and soakaway drainage fields.

  • Proper slope alignment to prevent backflow
  • Inspection manholes with airtight covers
  • Ballast soakaway pits and sub-surface French drains
