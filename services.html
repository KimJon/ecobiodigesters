Engineering Services & Solutions

Decentralized wastewater treatment infrastructure, commercial grease interception, biological septic retrofitting, and hydraulic civil drainage engineered for institutional, commercial, and residential developments.

Flagship Infrastructure

Sub-Surface Anaerobic Bio-Digesters

Our closed-loop, biological wastewater treatment systems deploy naturally occurring anaerobic bacterial digestion to decompose blackwater and organic effluent into clarified, pathogen-free water. Constructed using high-density reinforced expanded polystyrene (EPS) composite structural technology, our systems eliminate vacuum exhauster dependency and operate with complete atmospheric containment.

  • Autonomous continuous biological degradation — zero recurring vacuum evacuation required
  • Hermetically sealed containment ensuring 100% odourless and vector-free operation
  • 50+ year structural lifespan delivered by reinforced EPS composite matrix
  • Calibrated for challenging geotechnical profiles (expansive clays, murram, and volcanic bedrock)
  • Rapid turnkey commissioning within 1 to 7 working days from groundbreaking to handover
  • Ultra-compact sub-surface footprint allowing full overhead utilization for carports, driveways, or landscaping

Effluent Pre-Treatment

Passive Fat, Oil & Grease (FOG) Interceptors

Culinary fats, oils, and grease discharged from institutional kitchens and dining facilities rapidly solidify, causing severe sewer pipe occlusion and glazing over soakaway filtration substrates. Our engineered cast-in-place grease interceptors employ multi-chamber gravimetric separation to isolate FOG compounds before effluent enters the dispersal network.

  • High-efficiency multi-stage baffle separation preventing sewer conduit blockage
  • Heavy-duty airtight removable access lids for streamlined periodic maintenance
  • Engineered specifically for restaurants, hotels, educational cafeterias, and commercial kitchens
  • Sized precisely to match peak culinary flow volume and fixture discharge ratings
  • Safeguards sub-surface soakaway absorption fields against premature hydraulic failure

Infrastructure Remediation

Septic Infrastructure Modernization & Retrofitting

For commercial facilities or private properties burdened by failing, foul-smelling masonry septic tanks that require recurrent monthly pumping, structural demolition is entirely unnecessary. Our specialized engineering teams convert existing containment chambers into active, continuous multi-stage anaerobic bioreactors.

  • Complete terminal effluent evacuation and structural pressure wash decontamination
  • Civil construction of internal anaerobic baffle partitions and multi-chamber division walls
  • Re-engineering of PVC inlet and outlet manifolds with directional hydraulic overflow tees
  • Casting of reinforced airtight concrete slab covers with flush-level inspection manholes
  • Introduction of concentrated biological enzyme strains to establish immediate microbial digestion
  • Complete conversion from an obsolete holding tank to an autonomous, maintenance-free system

Civil Hydraulics

Hydraulic Reticulation & Sub-Surface Drainage

A bio-digestion plant performs only as effectively as the hydraulic reticulation that feeds and disperses its effluent. We design and execute complete site sewer networks, laser-verified gravitational gradient lines, inspection manholes, and sub-surface French drain absorption systems.

  • Heavy-duty Class 41 PVC sewer pipelines engineered for continuous gravitational flow
  • Precision spirit-level and laser-verified gradient calculation mitigating backflow risks
  • Monolithic inspection chambers and rodding access points at critical directional intersections
  • Perforated sub-surface French drain lines lined with permeable non-woven geotextile fabric
  • Executed and supervised by certified civil engineering and sanitation installation specialists
