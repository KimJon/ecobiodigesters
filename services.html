Our Services

Our Signature Service

Biodigester Installation

Our permanent, eco-friendly biodigester systems use naturally occurring anaerobic bacteria to break down human waste into clean water and harmless gases. Constructed using reinforced polystyrene technology, our systems never fill up and produce zero odour.

  • Never fills up — zero exhauster bowser visits needed
  • 100% odour-free — airtight sealed reinforced chambers
  • 50+ year durability with reinforced polystyrene technology
  • Engineered for all Kenyan soils (black cotton, rock, red loam)
  • Fast 1 to 7 working days installation timeline
  • Space-saving — safely installed under lawns, driveways or carports

Kitchen Wastewater Solutions

Grease Traps Installation

Cooking fats, oils, and food solids from kitchen sinks can easily solidify and clog your sewer lines or choke your soak pit. Our heavy-duty grease interceptors separate fats from water before it ever enters the drainage area.

  • Prevents severe drainage blockages and pipe clogging
  • Removable lid for easy, hassle-free periodic cleaning
  • Essential for restaurants, hotels, schools, and residential homes
  • Custom-sized to handle your specific cooking volume
  • Protects your soakaway pit from fatty grease coating

Remediation & Upgrades

Old Septic Tank Conversion

Do you have an existing traditional septic tank that fills up every few months or smells terribly? You do not have to demolish your compound! Our experienced team can convert your existing tank into a modern biological biodigester.

  • Exhaust and power-clean the old tank for the last time
  • Build internal baffle division walls into anaerobic chambers
  • Realign PVC inlet and outlet T-pipes for proper overflow
  • Cast an airtight reinforced top slab with inspection lids
  • Add high-potency bacterial enzymes to kickstart digestion
  • Permanent fix — no more exhauster trucks forever
